# Our Story — Tester Guide

Thank you for helping us test Our Story! This is a private space where families and friends build a shared photo timeline together.

This guide walks you through everything we'd like you to try. There are no right or wrong answers — if something feels confusing, slow, or broken, that's exactly what we want to hear.

---

## Before you start

**You'll need:**

- A phone or tablet (preferably both iPhone and Android if you have access)
- A computer (optional but useful)
- 2 email addresses you can check (one for you, one to invite — a friend or family member works perfectly, or any second email of your own)
- About 30 minutes total — you can spread it across a few sessions

**Test on:** ideally try the app on your phone first (it's designed for mobile), then briefly on your computer.

**How long this takes:** about 20–30 minutes for the main flow, plus 10 minutes for the optional sections at the end.

---

## How to report a problem

If anything goes wrong, please note down:

1. **What you were trying to do** (e.g., "I was uploading a photo")
2. **What happened instead** (e.g., "the upload spinner stayed forever")
3. **What you expected** (e.g., "I expected the photo to appear on the timeline")
4. **A screenshot** (very helpful!) — on iPhone press Side button + Volume up; on Android press Power + Volume down
5. **Which device and browser** (e.g., "iPhone 14, Safari")

You can send these notes by email — bundle them up at the end of the session rather than after each one.

**Big things to call out:**

- Anything that crashes, freezes, or shows an obvious error
- Words or buttons that don't make sense
- Confusing flows ("I didn't know what to do next")
- Anything that surprises you in a bad way

**Don't worry about:**

- Push notifications on iPhone — those don't work yet on iPhone web (we'll add them in a future version)
- Email notifications — these may take a few minutes to arrive, or may not arrive at all during testing (that's expected and we'll let you know)

## Part 1 — Getting started (5 min)

### 1.1 Visit the website

Open the link we sent you in your phone's browser.

- ✅ You should see a welcome page with "Start your circle — free" near the top
- ✅ Scroll down — you should see a "How it works" section, then cards for different kinds of circles (family, couple, friends, etc.), then a privacy promise

### 1.2 Sign up

Tap "Start your circle — free", or "Sign in" if you see it.

- Type in your email address
- Tap "Send magic link"
- Open your inbox on the same device — you should see an email from us within a minute
- Tap the link in the email

✅ You should be signed in automatically. If this is your first time, you'll be asked for your first and last name. Fill those in.

**Try to break it:**

- What happens if you type a typo'd email? (You shouldn't get a magic link — that's fine.)
- What happens if you wait too long before clicking the link?

### 1.3 Sign out, then sign back in

Find the menu (usually top-right) and sign out. Then sign in again with the same email.

- ✅ You should NOT have to fill in your name again
- ✅ You should land back where you left off

---

## Part 2 — Creating your first circle (5 min)

A "circle" is a private group — like a family, a couple, or a group of friends. You'll create one and invite people.

### 2.1 Pick a circle type

You should see a screen asking what kind of circle this is — Parents, Couple, Family, Friends, Travel, Solo, or "Other".

- Pick whichever feels right
- ✅ The card you tap should highlight
- Tap "Continue"

### 2.2 Name your circle

- Type a name (e.g., "The Smiths", "Tokyo Trip 2026", "Mom and Dad")
- Tap "Continue"

### 2.3 Invite someone

- Type the second email address (your friend's, or a second email you own)
- Tap "Send"
- ✅ You should see a "Sent to ..." confirmation
- Check that second inbox — the invite email should arrive within a minute

**Or:** if you're testing alone and don't want to invite anyone right now, tap "Skip for now". You can invite people later.

### 2.4 Accept the invite (other person)

Now switch to the second email address — open it on a different device, or open the invite link in a private/incognito window on the same device.

- Tap the link in the invite email
- ✅ You should see a brief "Joining..." screen
- If this is a brand new email, you'll be asked to sign up (sign up like in step 1.2)
- ✅ After signing in, you should land in the timeline of the circle you were invited to

## Part 3 — Adding memories (10 min)

You're now on the timeline. It's probably empty. Let's add some memories.

### 3.1 Upload a single photo

- Tap the "+" button (usually at the bottom of the screen)
- Tap "Photo or video"
- Pick a photo from your camera roll
- (Optional) add a note, a milestone label, or change the date
- Tap "Upload" or "Post"
- ✅ You should see an upload spinner, then the photo should appear on the timeline
- ✅ The photo should be slightly tilted, like a real polaroid

### 3.2 Upload a video

Same flow, but pick a video. Short videos work best for testing (under a minute).

- ✅ The video should appear on the timeline with a play indicator
- Tap it — it should open and let you play

### 3.3 Write a quick note (no photo)

Sometimes you just want to write a thought down without a photo.

- Tap "+", then tap "Quick note"
- Type something — anything ("First day of summer", "She said her first word!", whatever)
- (Optional) add a milestone label like "First word"
- Tap "Save"
- ✅ A new card appears on the timeline — it should look different from a photo (more like a postcard with a red pin)

### 3.4 Upload multiple photos at once

- Tap "+", then "Photo or video"
- Pick 3+ photos
- You should see a toggle: "Post as one memory" — try both options:

**Option A — Post as one memory:** Turn the toggle ON.

- (Optional) try adding a text slide to the group
- (Optional) drag photos to reorder, or set one as the cover
- Upload
- ✅ You should see ONE card on the timeline with a stack indicator (a small "+N" badge)
- Tap it — you should be able to swipe between the photos like a carousel

**Option B — Post separately:** Turn the toggle OFF.

- Upload again with different photos
- ✅ Each photo becomes its own card on the timeline

### 3.5 Edit a memory

Find a memory you uploaded.

- Open it (tap the card)
- Tap the pencil/edit icon (only visible on YOUR memories)
- Change the note, add a milestone, or change the date
- Save
- ✅ The card should update with your changes

**Note:** when looking at memories your friend uploaded, you should NOT see the pencil — only your own can be edited.

## Part 4 — Reactions, comments, and milestones (5 min)

### 4.1 Add a reaction

On a memory card, hover (on computer) or look for the "+" emoji button (on phone).

- Tap "+" and pick an emoji like ❤️
- ✅ The emoji should appear as a chip with a count of 1
- Tap the chip again
- ✅ The reaction should disappear (you can toggle your own off)

### 4.2 Add a comment

- Tap a memory to open it
- Find the comments section
- Type something and tap "Post" (or press Enter)
- ✅ Your comment should appear immediately

### 4.3 Edit your comment

- Hover over your comment (or tap it on mobile)
- Tap the pencil icon
- Change the text → save
- ✅ The comment updates and shows a small "edited" label

### 4.4 Delete your comment

- Tap the trash icon on your comment
- ✅ A confirmation appears ("Delete this comment?")
- Tap "Delete"
- ✅ The comment is removed

### 4.5 Add a milestone

- Upload a new photo (or edit an existing one)
- This time, tap one of the suggested milestone chips (like "First steps" or "Anniversary")
- (Or type your own custom milestone)
- Save
- ✅ The memory should appear with a ✦ badge showing the milestone

### 4.6 Milestone share card

After saving a memory with a milestone, you should see a popup offering a shareable card.

- ✅ The card shows your photo with the milestone label and a nice design
- Try toggling between square and tall (9:16) format
- Try "Share" — on phone you should get the native share sheet; on computer it'll download an image
- (You don't actually need to share it anywhere — just verify the card looks right)

## Part 5 — Sharing with people who don't want an account (5 min)

This is for inviting grandparents or relatives who just want to view photos without creating an account.

### 5.1 Create a share link

- On the timeline, look for a "Share link" button (usually in the header — only visible if you're the circle owner)
- Tap it → "Create new link"
- Pick "Full circle" (lets viewers see everything)
- Give it a name like "For Mom"
- Tap "Create"
- ✅ A link appears with copy / revoke / renew buttons

### 5.2 Open the share link

- Copy the link
- Open it in a private/incognito browser window (or a different browser, or send it to yourself on a different device)
- ✅ You should land on a viewer page WITHOUT being asked to sign in
- ✅ You should see the memories from the circle
- (If it's your first time on a viewer page, a welcome splash appears)

### 5.3 React as a guest

- Tap a reaction on the viewer page
- ✅ You'll be asked your name (first time)
- Type a name → confirm
- ✅ Your reaction sticks with that name attached

### 5.4 Revoke a share link

- Go back to your circle (the owner side) → share links sheet
- Tap the trash icon on a link → confirm
- Reload the viewer tab from step 5.2
- ✅ It should show an "expired" or "no longer available" state

## Part 6 — Notifications (3 min)

### 6.1 Push notifications (Android Chrome only — skip on iPhone)

On Android Chrome, after a couple of timeline visits you may see a banner asking to enable notifications.

- Tap "Enable" → allow when your browser asks
- Now have your second account upload a photo (or comment, or react)
- ✅ A notification should appear on your phone within a few seconds
- Tap it — it should open the relevant memory

**On iPhone:** push notifications don't work in the browser yet — this is a known limit. Skip this section.

### 6.2 Notification settings

- Find the bell icon in the timeline header (or open the menu → notifications)
- ✅ You should see toggles for each circle: mute, push, milestone reminders, and email frequency (weekly/monthly/off)
- Try toggling some — the settings should save

### 6.3 Mute a circle

- Mute one of your circles
- Have your other account upload a photo there
- ✅ You should NOT get a push notification (because you muted it)
- Unmute and try again — push should come through

---

## Part 7 — Personalization (3 min)

### 7.1 Change your name

- Go to Settings → Account
- Change your first/last name → save
- ✅ Your name should update wherever it appears (comments, reactions, etc.)

### 7.2 Switch language

- Find the language picker (in the header menu or your profile)
- Switch English → 中文 (Chinese) → Français (French) → back to English
- ✅ All the words on the screen should change immediately
- ✅ Dates should change format too (e.g., May 11, 2026 → 2026年5月11日 → 11 mai 2026)
- Sign out and back in
- ✅ The language you picked should stick

### 7.3 Edit circle name

- If you're the owner, open Circle Settings
- Change the circle name → save
- ✅ The new name should show everywhere (timeline header, the circle switcher, future emails)

### 7.4 Change circle type

- In Circle Settings, you should see a grid of circle types
- Pick a different one → "Save"
- ✅ The empty-state copy and milestone suggestions update to match

### 7.5 Add a child (Parents circle only)

- In Circle Settings, find the children section
- Add a child with name + date of birth
- Now upload a photo and tag that child
- ✅ The photo card should show a pill with the child's name and their age at that time (e.g., "Emma · 3 months, 2 weeks")

## Part 8 — Phone polish (5 min — phone only)

### 8.1 Install to Home Screen

After visiting the app a few times, you should see a banner offering to "install" the app.

**On Android:**

- Tap "Install"
- ✅ The browser asks to confirm → confirm
- ✅ An icon appears on your home screen
- Tap the icon
- ✅ The app opens without browser UI (looks like a native app)

**On iPhone:**

- The banner shows instructions: "Tap Share → Add to Home Screen"
- Follow them
- ✅ An icon appears on your home screen
- Open the app from the icon
- ✅ It opens in full-screen mode

### 8.2 Multiple circles

If you have access to multiple circles (or create a second one):

- Look for a circle switcher in the header
- Switch between them
- ✅ The timeline reloads with each circle's memories

## Part 9 — Optional advanced tests (5 min)

### 9.1 Export your data

- Settings → Account → "Export my data"
- Pick a circle → start export
- ✅ You should see "Export queued" or similar
- Within a few minutes, an email arrives with a download link
- Open the link — you should get a zip file with photos + a manifest
- (The link expires after 24 hours)

### 9.2 Leave a circle / delete your account

**⚠️ Only do this with a test account you don't mind losing!**

- Settings → Account → "Delete my account"
- You should be given options:
  - "Keep my content as a Former Member" — your photos stay, your name shows as "Former Member"
  - "Remove all my content" — your photos are deleted
- Pick one → confirm
- ✅ You should be signed out

### 9.3 Delete a circle (owner only)

**⚠️ Only do this with a test circle you don't mind losing!**

- Circle Settings → "Delete circle" (this is usually at the bottom, in red)
- You'll be asked to type the circle name to confirm
- Confirm
- ✅ The circle goes into a 30-day grace period; you and members are notified
- ✅ If you log in within 30 days, you can cancel the deletion

## Part 10 — Things to look out for everywhere

While you're going through the above, please also keep an eye out for:

- **Slow pages** — does anything take more than 3 seconds to load?
- **Words that look like code** — like `t('common.someone')` or `{{ name }}` — those are bugs
- **Buttons that don't work** when you tap them
- **Photos that don't load** or look pixelated when they shouldn't
- **Text overflowing** off the screen or being cut off
- **Empty states that feel wrong** (e.g., "you have 0 memories" worded harshly)
- **Spelling and grammar** — point out anything that reads oddly
- **iPhone-specific quirks** — does the bottom of the screen get covered by the home indicator? Do dialogs feel right?
- **Dark mode** — if your device has dark mode on, do colors look right?
