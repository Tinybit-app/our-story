Part 10 — General things to watch for

  • ☐ Slow pages (anything over 3 seconds)
  • ☐ Code-like text (e.g., t('common.someone'), {{ name }})
  • ☐ Buttons that don't respond when tapped
  • ☐ Photos that fail to load or look pixelated
  • ☐ Text overflowing off the screen
  • ☐ Empty states that feel harsh
  • ☐ Spelling and grammar issues
  • ☐ iPhone home-indicator overlap on bottom sheets
  • ☐ Dark mode: colors look wrong if your device is in dark mode
